حروف کی شکلیں

Right-joining forms ا د ڈ ذ ر ڑ ز ژ و ے can connect to the preceding letter on their right.

Special forms ں marks a nasal vowel at the end of a word, as in ماں. Standalone ء has no joins. Seated hamza ئ can join, as in نائی.

Aspirated pairs

The letter ھ adds a puff of air. Compare aspirated کھ with the throaty خ. After د and ڈ, the pair still spells one sound even though the letters stay separate.
